## Kanji and vocabulary data

Readings, meanings, stroke counts, radicals, school grades and frequency ranks
derive from **KANJIDIC2**; vocabulary glosses derive from **JMdict**. Both are the
work of the [Electronic Dictionary Research and Development Group](https://www.edrdg.org/)
and are used under the
[Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 licence](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).

## Example sentences

Japanese–English sentence pairs come from the **Tanaka corpus**, released into the
public domain by Yasuhito Tanaka, corrected and maintained by the EDRDG and by the
[Tatoeba Project](https://tatoeba.org/), and used under the
[Creative Commons Attribution 2.0 France licence](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/2.0/fr/).

Sentences are reproduced unmodified. The kana readings and romaji shown beneath
each one are generated by this project using [UniDic](https://clrd.ninjal.ac.jp/unidic/)
morphological analysis, and are not part of the corpus.

## Stroke-order diagrams

Stroke paths come from [**KanjiVG**](https://github.com/KanjiVG/kanjivg) by Ulrich
Apel and contributors, used under the
[Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 3.0 licence](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/).

The SVGs are vendored into this repository under `data/stroke/` so the site builds
offline. At build time they are recoloured to follow the page's theme and stripped
of metadata that is not rendered; the stroke paths and KanjiVG's own stroke
numbering are unmodified.

## Level lists

JLPT level membership is cross-checked against
[kanji.jepang.org](https://kanji.jepang.org/) and
[kanjilibrary.com](https://kanjilibrary.com/). Neither site is affiliated with this
project or with the JLPT. See [Sources]({{ '/about/sources/' | relative_url }}) for how the two are reconciled.

## Software

Built with [MkDocs](https://www.mkdocs.org/) and the
[Material for MkDocs](https://squidfunk.github.io/mkdocs-material/) theme. Every
page under a level is generated from the YAML in `data/kanji/` at build time.

## Not affiliated with the JLPT

The Japanese-Language Proficiency Test is administered by the
[Japan Foundation](https://www.jlpt.jp/) and Japan Educational Exchanges and
Services. This is an independent study site with no connection to either
organisation, and nothing here is an official statement of what the exam covers.
